Gearless PMSM Traction Machine

Kinematic & Engineering Function

Converts electrical power into smooth rotational torque to drive suspension ropes without mechanical reduction gear losses.

Technical Tolerances & Parameters

Efficiency: >94%, Torque: up to 1,600 Nm, Rare-earth NdFeB permanent magnets, Dual independent disc brakes.

Traction vs. MRL Comparison:

Conventional overhead traction utilizes a dedicated penthouse machine room and supports velocities up to 12.5 m/s. Machine Room-Less (MRL) mounts a compact gearless motor inside the hoistway head, reclaiming building leasable square footage for travels up to 90 m.
