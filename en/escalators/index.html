Rigid Structural Steel Truss

Engineering Function

Engineered box-girder truss supporting all functional mechanics, drive units, step tracks, and live passenger loads with deflection strictly limited to 1/750 of span.

Distinctive Engineering Feature

Galvanized hollow structural sections with acoustic damping pads

Eco-Crawl™ Intelligent Idling

When passenger flow ceases for over 30 seconds, dual Doppler radars decelerate the drive to 0.1 m/s, curtailing standby power consumption by up to 60% without mechanical wear.

Zero-Harm Safety Interlock Architecture

Every VERTEX escalator integrates over 32 independent safety monitoring switches including comb plate deflector sensors, step sag detectors, broken step chain interlocks, and synchronized handrail speed sensors.
