Permanent Magnet Synchronous Machines (PMSM)

High-torque, low-RPM gearless machines with rare-earth neodymium magnets operate at over 94% mechanical efficiency with zero oil lubricants and near-silent operation.

PILLAR 03

Regenerative Power Feedback

When an empty car travels upward or a loaded car travels downward, gravitational potential energy is converted back into 3-phase clean AC electrical power, returned directly to the building grid.

Traction vs. MRL Comparison:

Conventional overhead traction utilizes a dedicated penthouse machine room and supports velocities up to 12.5 m/s. Machine Room-Less (MRL) mounts a compact gearless motor inside the hoistway head, reclaiming building leasable square footage for travels up to 90 m.
